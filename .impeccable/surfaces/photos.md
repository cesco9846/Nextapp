# Foto del trattamento

Mode: Operate. Narrow extension of Glow clinical dossier, inheriting DESIGN.md and PRODUCT.md.
THESIS: acquisisci e confronta prima/dopo/guarito sapendo per quale seduta e quali usi la cliente ha autorizzato le immagini.
OWN-WORLD: Glow jade actions, cool ground, white surfaces; Bricolage headings, Instrument Sans body, shadcn/ui and 44px controls. Real client photos only; no decorative raster.
STORY: apri il dossier, scegli la seduta, verifica le due autorizzazioni firmate, comprimi e carica una foto; apri le immagini private e scegli due categorie da confrontare. Revoca esplicita per usi futuri.
FIRST VIEWPORT: back link and client name, appointment selector, compact permission status. Upload follows with category before file picker. Gallery is the main visual surface once images exist.
FORM: responsive single column, wider gallery/compare on desktop. No draggable-only interaction. No persistent image/token cache, no offline queue, explicit retry keeps exact in-memory upload attempt. Missing authorization has a preparation link; HEIC error explains JPEG/PNG. Historical images remain private after revocation, archive is not deletion. Optional photo choices in signature are unchecked and distinct from treatment confirmation.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
